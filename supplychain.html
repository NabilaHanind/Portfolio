Supply Chain Analytics

Global Supply Chain & Procurement Dashboard

An end-to-end data analytics and procurement optimization platform simulating enterprise logistics, real-time supplier performance tracking, and order fulfillment workflows.

Role: Supply Chain Systems Analyst
Focus: Procurement, Inventory & S/4HANA Integration
Dataset: 20,000+ Sales & Procurement Records
Dataset & Model (.xlsx)

1. Business Context & Objective

In modern manufacturing and distribution networks, inefficient procurement and delayed shipments directly impact operational costs and customer satisfaction. This project simulates an enterprise supply chain environment—mirroring SAP S/4HANA data structures—to track supplier reliability, monitor order statuses, and evaluate profit margins across global regions.

2. Analytics & Procurement Dashboard

The interactive dashboard provides logistics managers with real-time visibility into on-time delivery rates, late delivery risk flags, economic value, and extracted material costs across global operations.

Executive Dashboard: Real-time Tracking of Logistics & Circular Economy Metrics

3. Value & Enterprise Alignment

By bridging raw operational data with actionable dashboards, this system demonstrates how businesses can proactively mitigate delivery bottlenecks, optimize preferred supplier partnerships, and streamline order-to-cash workflows—aligning closely with enterprise resource planning (ERP) best practices.
